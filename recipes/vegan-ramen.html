Vegan Ramen Recipe



Description

Craving for ramen one day, I decided to find a recipe that was vegan to test out if it was possible to create a broth of deliciousness without meat. Here's my in-progress recipe:

  • 2 tbsp finely chopped king oyster mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp chopped green onion
  • 1/2 diced onion
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp chili bean sauce
  • 2 tbsp miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 400ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp sesame paste
  • pinch of white pepper
  • 1 tsp ground sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 50g sliced cabbage
  • optional soya milk

Toppings

  • grilled king oyster mushrooms
  • sprouts
  • green onion
  • black fungus
  • menma
  • nori
  • ajitama

Steps

  1. Fry aromatics: garlic, onion.
  2. Deglaze with stock.
